VivaGraph examples

Each page is a script tag and a few lines; view its source. To open them from a checkout, build the library first (npm run build).

  • Real graphs, each loaded from a JSON file: the London Underground, who influenced whom, the royal houses of Europe, Greek mythology, programming languages, Nobel laureates and their teachers, air routes, constellations, muscles and bones, the Burgess Shale food web, Les Misérables, word ladders, countries and their borders, US counties, mammals, chess openings, languages of the world, metros of the world and 74,000 English nouns.
  • Hello: four nodes, one call.
  • Colors from the data: 240 people in six teams, with a legend.
  • Light and dark: a white background, and a button that switches.
  • 20,000 nodes: on WebGPU where the device has one.
  • 3D: a cube of 1,728 nodes.
  • A graph that grows: tap a node to add more.
